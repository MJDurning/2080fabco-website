03How a project works

From napkin sketch to finished part.

  1. 1

    Send the idea

    A description, a photo, a sketch or rough measurements is enough to start. A broken part works too.

  2. 2

    Design and agree

    We work out the approach together, model it in CAD where it helps, and confirm materials, size and scope before anything is built.

  3. 3

    Build in-house

    Printing, cutting, wiring, engraving and finishing happen in the Delco shop, with fit checks along the way.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    You get the finished piece and, where it applies, the files that made it.

05About 2080 Fab Co.

A builder’s approach to modern fabrication.

2080 Fabrications Co. is a small maker shop in Delaware County, Pennsylvania, started in 2025. It combines hands-on craftsmanship with modern tools to create useful, durable and highly customized products.

Whether the project begins as a napkin sketch, a broken part, a rough measurement or an idea that has never existed before, the goal is the same: design it intelligently, build it carefully and deliver something worth using.
